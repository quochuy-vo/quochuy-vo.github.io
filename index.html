About

Quoc-Huy Vo is a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Informatics at SOKENDAI (The Graduate University for Advanced Studies), carrying out research at the National Institute of Informatics (NII) in Tokyo, Japan, under the supervision of Prof. Kensuke Fukuda. He is currently a Visiting Doctoral Researcher at the AaltoSEA lab at Aalto University, Finland, working with Prof. Linh Truong.

His research interests lie in Cloud Computing, Information Security, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), and Web Privacy. Specifically, he develops automated static analysis frameworks and leverages fine-tuned Large Language Models (LLMs) to detect security vulnerabilities, anti-patterns, and code smells in Terraform configurations. In parallel, he conducts empirical web privacy measurements analyzing Personally Identifiable Information (PII) leakage across Single Sign-On (SSO) ecosystems.

Prior to his doctoral studies, he worked for over three years as a DevOps and Back-End Engineer at FPT Software and Meete.co. He received his Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Network and Data Communication from the University of Information Technology.
